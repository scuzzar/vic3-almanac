Fleet Almanac

Thinking about taking on Great Britain? Better take a look at the Royal Navy first.
How many capital ships are waiting for you, and have they already switched to dreadnoughts? Is their fleet modern or still waiting for a refit - and where is it right now?

Fleet Almanac gives you the answers at a glance: it adds an "Almanac" tab to the country panel of every nation, including your own, with a compact overview of its navy.

UNIT TYPES
- The newest unlocked infantry, artillery and cavalry type of the nation at a glance, with the unit type tooltip

SHIPS
- Ships grouped into Capital Ships, Cruisers and Torpedo Craft
- One line per ship template: number of ships (and ships under construction), ship type, template name, modifications, armor and damage
- Modifications are always shown in the same order, so templates are easy to compare
- A marker below the ship count shows outdated ships of a template; its tooltip lists every outdated ship with its actual equipment

FLEETS
- Main fleets first, small fleets (less than 5% of the navy or fewer than 3 ships) listed separately below
- One compact line per fleet: name, status and current location, ships per group, outdated ships and a button to open the fleet
- Expand a fleet to see the same ship template overview for that fleet

MAP
- While hovering the Almanac, the map switches to the military map mode (fleets, HQs, naval missions)

COMPATIBILITY
- User interface only: no gameplay changes, nothing is saved to your game
- Safe to add or remove mid-game
- Not required for multiplayer by other players
- Overrides the country panel, so it may conflict with other mods that change the country panel
- Built for Victoria 3 1.13 (also works on the 1.14 beta)

Languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese
